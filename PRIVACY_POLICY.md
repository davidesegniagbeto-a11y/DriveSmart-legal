# Privacy Policy for DriveSmart UK

**Last Updated: December 28, 2025**

DriveSmart UK ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Personal Information
When you register for an account, we collect:
- **Name** (first and last)
- **Email address**
- **Phone number**
- **Role** (Learner Driver or Driving Instructor)
- **Password** (stored encrypted)

### 1.2 Location Data
We collect precise location data when you use the App:
- **Foreground Location**: When actively using driving session features
- **Background Location**: During active driving sessions to track your route (only when explicitly started by you)
- **Purpose**: To analyze driving patterns, generate practice routes, and provide feedback

### 1.3 Motion and Sensor Data
We collect data from your device's sensors:
- **Accelerometer**: To detect sudden braking, acceleration, and turns
- **Gyroscope**: To analyze vehicle handling and stability
- **GPS Speed**: To monitor driving speed and adherence to limits
- **Purpose**: To provide real-time feedback and generate driving reports

### 1.4 Driving Session Data
When you record a driving session, we collect:
- Route coordinates (GPS trajectory)
- Timestamps and duration
- Detected driving events (harsh braking, speeding, etc.)
- Speed data
- Distance traveled

### 1.5 Usage Data
We automatically collect:
- Device information (model, operating system version)
- App usage statistics
- Crash reports and error logs
- Session frequency and duration

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

### 2.1 Core Functionality
- **User Authentication**: To create and manage your account
- **Driving Analysis**: To provide personalized feedback on your driving
- **Route Suggestions**: To generate practice routes based on your skill level using AI
- **Progress Tracking**: To monitor your improvement over time
- **Booking Management**: To facilitate lesson scheduling between learners and instructors

### 2.2 Service Improvement
- **Analytics**: To understand how users interact with the App
- **Bug Fixes**: To identify and resolve technical issues
- **Feature Development**: To improve existing features and develop new ones

### 2.3 Communications
- **Account Updates**: Important notices about your account
- **Booking Confirmations**: Notifications about lesson bookings
- **Safety Alerts**: Critical updates about app functionality

## 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

### 3.1 With Your Instructor (Learners Only)
If you are a learner driver, we share your driving session data with your assigned instructor to:
- Review your progress
- Provide personalized feedback
- Plan future lessons

### 3.2 With Your Learners (Instructors Only)
If you are an instructor, we provide access to your learners':
- Booking history
- Session reports
- Progress statistics

### 3.3 Third-Party Service Providers
We use the following third-party services:
- **Mapbox**: For map rendering and geocoding services
- **OpenAI**: For AI-powered route suggestions and driving insights
- **Neon (PostgreSQL)**: For secure database hosting

These providers have access only to data necessary for their services and are obligated to protect it.

### 3.4 Legal Requirements
We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to:
- Court orders or legal processes
- Protection of our rights or safety
- Investigation of fraud or security issues

### 3.5 Business Transfers
If DriveSmart UK is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred. We will notify you before your data is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

## 4. Data Retention

- **Active Accounts**: We retain your data while your account is active
- **Inactive Accounts**: Data is retained for 2 years after last activity, then anonymized
- **Driving Sessions**: Retained indefinitely for progress tracking (unless you request deletion)
- **Deleted Accounts**: Data is permanently deleted within 30 days of account deletion request

## 5. Your Rights (GDPR Compliance)

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have the right to:

### 5.1 Access Your Data
Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you via the "Export My Data" feature in the app.

### 5.2 Rectification
Update or correct inaccurate information through your profile settings.

### 5.3 Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")
Request deletion of your account and all associated data via the "Delete Account" feature.

### 5.4 Data Portability
Download your data in a machine-readable format (JSON).

### 5.5 Restrict Processing
Request that we limit how we use your data.

### 5.6 Object to Processing
Object to certain uses of your personal information.

### 5.7 Withdraw Consent
Withdraw consent for location tracking at any time (will prevent driving session recording).

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@drivesmart.uk or use the in-app features.

## 6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:
- **Encryption in Transit**: All data transmitted using TLS/SSL
- **Encryption at Rest**: Database encryption for sensitive data
- **Password Security**: Passwords hashed using bcrypt
- **Access Controls**: Role-based access to user data
- **Regular Audits**: Security reviews and updates

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Children's Privacy

DriveSmart UK is intended for users aged 17 and older (minimum age to hold a provisional driving license in the UK). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 17. If we discover we have collected data from a child, we will delete it immediately.

## 8. Location Data Specifics

### 8.1 When We Collect Location
- **Foreground**: Only when the app is open and visible
- **Background**: Only during active driving sessions that you explicitly start
- **Never**: When the app is closed or no session is active

### 8.2 How to Disable
You can disable location access in your device settings at any time. This will prevent driving session recording.

### 8.3 Background Location Justification
Background location is essential for:
- Continuous route tracking during driving sessions
- Accurate detection of driving events
- Complete session data even when the phone is locked

## 9. Cookies and Tracking

We do not use cookies in the mobile app. We do use:
- **Analytics**: To understand app usage patterns
- **Crash Reporting**: To identify and fix bugs

You can opt out of analytics tracking via the in-app settings.

## 10. International Data Transfers

Your data is stored on servers located in the European Union (Neon/AWS EU-West-2). If you access the app from outside the EU, your data will be transferred to EU servers with appropriate safeguards.

- In-app notification

Your continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
